A referral counts only when a different user enters through your link and performs a qualifying action such as Save Draft, Preview, Print or Export.

How the referral scheme works

1

Use one module

Choose any EasyFile module and complete one meaningful document action. This consumes the once-off free use.

2

Refer three people

Share your unique link with three different people. Self-referrals and repeat use by the same referral do not count twice.

3

Unlock continued access

When all three referrals qualify, the referral service unlocks continued access to the full EasyFile module suite.

Important

Referral validation requires an internet connection. Once an account is unlocked, its entitlement is cached so modules can continue operating during temporary service interruptions.
